
#### Correlation Rules

##### Flare - Critical

- Runs every 10 minutes over Flare events matched in the last 24 hours, instead of real time. A backfilled event keeps its Flare match time, so a real-time rule never opened an issue for it.

##### Flare - High

- Runs every 10 minutes over Flare events matched in the last 24 hours.
